Handoff: Household Consumption Forecast                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Status: Design only; no code or files created.                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Goal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Forecast whole-home consumption for the next 24 hours, producing both a five-minute watts profile and hourly kWh totals.                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Available data and constraints                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 - About three years of actual household consumption—direct home use, not grid import/export.                                                                                                                                               
 - Historical outdoor temperature data.                                                                                                                                                                                                     
 - The consumption data’s sampling interval and units are not yet confirmed.                                                                                                                                                                
 - Preferred data separation: train on historical actual consumption and historical actual temperature; use temperature forecasts only as inputs when making future predictions. Do not train on historical forecasts.                      
 - This model is separate from the PV-generation model. Their predictions could later be combined to estimate net load.                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Candidate modeling approach                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Start with a simple time-of-week baseline, then compare it with a weather-aware ML model. A gradient-boosted tree model is a reasonable candidate for learning nonlinear temperature and schedule effects; the algorithm is not yet        
 finalized.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Potential features:                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 - Target time: time of day, day of week, weekend/holiday, season.                                                                                                                                                                          
 - Recent consumption and comparable historical periods, such as the same time yesterday or last week.                                                                                                                                      
 - Historical outdoor temperature during training; future forecast temperature at inference.                                                                                                                                                
 - Temperature lags or rolling averages, since heating and cooling demand may respond gradually.                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 The model predicts the expected household load. Five-minute predictions can reflect the learned routine, but cannot predict the exact timing of unscheduled appliance events. Hourly kWh should generally be the more reliable planning    
 output.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Data handling and validation                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 - If the consumption history is five-minute, use it directly as five-minute targets. If it is coarser, a five-minute profile would need to be estimated rather than learned directly.                                                      
 - Confirm whether readings are instantaneous watts, interval-average watts, or energy per interval before converting predictions to kWh.                                                                                                   
 - Align temperature and consumption timestamps consistently; use a chronological train/validation split rather than a random split.                                                                                                        
 - Evaluate five-minute power error and hourly/daily energy error. Compare the ML model against the time-of-week baseline.                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Open questions                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 - What is the consumption history’s sampling interval and unit?                                                                                                                                                                            
 - Does the home have significant heating/cooling loads or scheduled high-use devices that should be represented?                                                                                                                           
 - What weather source and site coordinates will provide future temperature inputs?                                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
 Weather note: Adelaide was the location used for the earlier Open-Meteo research. Use the home’s coordinates and consistent timezone handling for any future weather requests.
